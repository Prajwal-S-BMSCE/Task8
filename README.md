The West region recorded the highest total sales, especially in Q3, indicating strong market performance and potential for expansion.

Technology was the top-performing category, contributing the highest share of total sales and showing consistent profit growth month-over-month.

Sales peaked in December, suggesting seasonal trends or successful year-end promotions that could be capitalized on in future campaigns.

Central and South regions showed relatively lower sales, which may highlight opportunities for targeted marketing or localized product offerings.
